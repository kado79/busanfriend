Why we're doing this

Most travel services show you a city. We'd like you to feel it — local culture as it really is: what people here do for fun, and what they actually eat.

My Korean Friend is a small test. We're stepping away from the usual tours and packages to try something simpler and more personal.

So a host here isn't someone who lays on activities for you to consume. A host shares their own hobbies, the places they think are wonderful and the restaurants they love — and builds the trip with you, so you enjoy it together.

The goal: experience Busan properly, the way the people who live here do.
